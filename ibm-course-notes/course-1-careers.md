## 5-Line Summary

1. This course introduced the cybersecurity industry and different job roles
2. A SOC has L1 analysts who check alerts first, L2 who investigate deeper, L3 for advanced threats
3. Offensive security finds holes (pen testers). Defensive security monitors and defends (SOC analysts)
4. As an L1 SOC analyst I will watch dashboards, triage alerts, and escalate real threats to L2
5. Interview question: What does a SOC analyst do? What is the difference between L1 and L2?

## Key Things I Learned

- SOC = Security Operations Centre — team that watches for attacks 24/7
- Threat: anything that could harm a system
- Vulnerability: a weakness that could be exploited
- Incident: a confirmed security problem
- L1 Analyst: first response. L2: investigates deeper. L3: advanced threats
